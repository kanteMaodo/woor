## Prompts de génération

### Commerce à Dakar

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full-width premium website hero background for Wóor, a protected payment service for local online commerce in Dakar, Senegal. Create one cinematic wide landscape photograph, 3:2 or wider composition. Scene: a modern small fashion boutique opening onto a sunlit Dakar street; a confident young Senegalese female shop owner in an elegant terracotta linen outfit, with short natural hair, is handing a neatly wrapped small kraft parcel to a Senegalese male motorcycle courier in a dark green overshirt, helmet tucked under one arm. They are candid, warm, professional, not looking at camera. Place the people and parcel in the right half of the photograph. Left half should show a naturally shadowed dark forest-green shop interior wall and subtle fabric shelving, with a large uncluttered dark area suitable for white webpage headlines overlaid later. A realistic small leafy plant, warm oak shop counter, beautiful woven fabrics in muted indigo and warm ochre at the far right, gentle sunlight on faces. Premium editorial lifestyle photography, authentic contemporary Dakar, realistic human anatomy and natural skin texture, subtle analog grain, true colors, soft highlights, deep forest shadows, 35mm lens. No overlaid text, no logos, no UI, no numbers, no artificial gradients or vignette, no watermark. This is an illustrative scene, not a photograph of real users.

A second young Senegalese woman customer is smiling in the softly focused background. Rich deep green painted walls, natural afternoon sunlight from a tall window, tasteful contemporary shop with tailoring tools and subtle clothes rail. Cinematic editorial photography, natural skin texture, authentic lived-in detail, warm neutral daylight, no stock-photo posed smiles, no text, no logos, no watermark, no gradients, landscape composition. Subjects occupy the right half with dark quiet green wall and shadows at the left for future white headline overlay. This is an illustration of commerce, not a real customer testimonial.
